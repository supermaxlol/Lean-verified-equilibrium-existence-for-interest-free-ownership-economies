Can an economy run on ownership instead of interest?

We proved that it can, and a computer checked every step: 195 Lean 4 modules, 2,113 audited results, using only the three standard axioms of mathematics.

The 50-year-old Islamic finance maths problem

For fifty years Islamic finance has priced against interest benchmarks such as LIBOR and SOFR, because no one had shown that an economy built on ownership and profit-sharing, with no interest rate, can clear. This work proves that it can.

What was achieved

  • Markets clear without interest. People hold shares, not loans. Prices are positive, budgets balance, every market clears.
  • Time still has value. It comes from people's patience, not from a lending rate.
  • A guaranteed premium adds nothing. A fixed return on top of a share creates no extra value and no better incentive.
  • When debt appears, and why. When people differ in how much risk they can bear.
  • Exact settlement. Profit shares and fees are paid to the last cent, with nothing lost.

Why it matters

For Islamic finance, a price-setting rule that borrows from no interest benchmark. For an automated economy, where machines earn more of the income, proof that an economy of owners can clear. For economics, the loan contract removed and equilibrium still found.
